Common questions

What does “Taken with Certifyr” mean?

Taken with Certifyr. App-attested capture. Doesn’t prove what the scene shows.

The Certifyr app sent a small record of the shot within minutes, and the uploaded original matched it exactly. How Certifyr works

Why didn’t my file match?

A check matches only the exact same file (or a copy Certifyr made): every byte has to be identical. Common reasons a copy doesn’t match:

  • It was converted, resized or compressed. Many apps do this when you send, post or save a photo — for example, turning HEIC into JPEG.
  • It’s a screenshot, a crop or an edited version.
  • Its original was never uploaded, was deleted, or its owner turned off lookup for it.
  • It wasn’t taken with the Certifyr camera. Most photos and videos aren’t.

For a photo, Search for Copies can still find a compressed, cropped or edited copy of a registered photo that can be looked up. It searches Certifyr’s records only, not the web.

No record doesn’t mean it’s fake. If you know who shared it, ask them for the original file or a Certifyr share link.

How long are originals hosted?

On the free plan, an uploaded original and its preview are hosted for 7 days, then deleted. The evidence record stays, and its page then says the original is no longer hosted. Certifyr isn’t a backup service, so keep your own copies.

You can upload 2 a day, reset at midnight UTC; a photo counts as 1 and a video as 2. Photos can be up to 25 MB and 48 megapixels; videos up to 15 seconds, 1080p and 50 MB. Failed uploads and retries don’t count again, and checking a file never does.

How do I delete my account?

In the Certifyr app, tap the thumbnail next to the shutter, then All at the top right (if a photo opens), then Settings (the gear), then Signed in with Apple at the top, then Delete Account. Deleting your account:

  • signs you out and stops evidence recording on your devices;
  • deletes your hosted originals and previews;
  • turns off your share links;
  • stops your records from being looked up by anyone.

Photos and videos saved on your iPhone aren’t deleted; delete them in the app, or delete the app. Your records are erased 30 days later. No longer have the app? Email jamesgui111@gmail.com.

How do I remove one photo or video from Certifyr?

Open it in the app’s library and tap the trash button. This deletes it from your iPhone and from Certifyr: its hosted copy and share links go away, and it can’t be looked up anymore. To keep it but stop others looking it up, open its Info and turn off Discoverable. To turn off one share link, tap Stop Sharing in Info.

How do I report abuse?

Use Report a Problem at the bottom of any record or share page — no account needed. For a name shown as “Shared by”, use Report next to it. You’ll get a reference to quote if you contact us. Or email jamesgui111@gmail.com with the record ID; you don’t need to send the share link.

We review every report. When something breaks our Terms of Use or the law, we may delete hosted media, turn off share links, stop a record from being looked up, or suspend an account. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

Does Certifyr upload my photos?

Your photos aren’t sent to Certifyr unless you choose to upload. With evidence on, the app sends a small record for each photo or video — the file’s fingerprint, the capture time and the device model — not the photo. Checking a file on the certifyr.app website sends only its fingerprint. Search for Copies sends the photo only when you tap it, and it’s deleted after the search. Privacy Policy

Why does a capture say “Saved locally — capture registration unavailable”?

Evidence can only be recorded within minutes of the shot, while you’re signed in, online, and using an iPhone that supports App Attest. Photos and videos taken otherwise are saved normally, but they never get capture evidence later — on purpose, so evidence always means the same thing.
