Privacy Policy

Checking a file sends only its fingerprint. A photo is sent only when you choose.

Last updated October 5, 2026

Who we are

Certifyr — the iPhone app and the certifyr.app website — is provided by RDVU CONNECT LLC(“we”). This policy explains what Certifyr collects, why, who handles it, and how to delete it.

When you check a file

  • Only the fingerprint is sent.Your browser reads the file and computes its SHA-256 fingerprint on your device. On the certifyr.app website, the request to Certifyr contains that fingerprint and nothing else — not the file, its name, its size or its type.
  • In the app, a check while signed in is tied to your account.The app sends the fingerprint with your sign-in, so your own private records can match. The result, including the fingerprint, is kept with your account for up to one hour so the app can show it again.
  • Comparing with a record happens on your device.On a record or share page, “Compare your copy” fingerprints your file locally and compares it with the record. Nothing about your file is sent. On a share page, your browser may download the shared preview again to compare it too.
  • Results are kept briefly.Certifyr keeps a check result for up to one hour so it can be shown to you; after that it can’t be retrieved and is deleted.
  • Like any website, your connection is visible.Your IP address and browser details reach Certifyr’s servers with every request. They are used to prevent abuse and keep the service secure.

When you search for copies

  • Only when you choose.After a check finds no match, you can tap Search for Copies. Only then is the photo itself sent to Certifyr — the exact file you chose. On the certifyr.app website the search is anonymous and the file’s name isn’t sent. A normal check still sends only the fingerprint.
  • Analyzed, then deleted.Certifyr looks for its invisible watermark in the photo, compares the picture with registered photos that can be looked up, and runs an automatic check for sensitive content. The photo is deleted right after — at the latest within two hours if something goes wrong. It is never used to train anything and never written to logs.
  • The result is kept briefly.The photo’s fingerprint, size and type, and which record matched, if any, are kept for up to one hour so the result can be shown to you, then deleted.
  • Searches are counted.To prevent abuse, each network address may search a limited number of times a day. The count is stored with a one-way code of the address, not the address itself, and deleted within three days.

The certifyr.app website

  • No cookies, analytics or trackers.The site doesn’t set cookies, doesn’t use analytics, and loads no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • Links don’t leak.Pages tell your browser not to send the page address to other sites, so share links aren’t passed along when you follow a link or load an image.
  • Reports keep only what you write.Report a Problem sends the reason, any details and the email you choose to give, plus which record or link you reported. No account is needed. Certifyr uses it only to review the report and erases the details and email 90 days after the case is closed.

What a public record shows

Anyone who has the exact file, or the record’s ID, can see its public record. Owners can turn this off for each photo or video, or for all of them.

  • ShownThe kind of evidence and its status; photo or video, format, dimensions and length; camera and lens; the capture time and device details as reported by the device; the times Certifyr recorded the evidence and received the original; whether the original is available; and the original’s fingerprint and size.
  • Shown only if the owner adds itA name and links (such as an Instagram username or a website) that the owner chose to show as “Shared by”. They’re added by the owner; Certifyr doesn’t confirm who they are.
  • Never shownThe owner’s Apple account ID, IP addresses, or other records; and their account number or device identifiers (except in an evidence file the owner includes).
  • LocationCertifyr never asks for your location. An existing photo or video you upload keeps the details already in its file, which can include where it was taken. Certifyr stores that file with your account, and anyone you let download the original gets them.
  • Originals and previewsA public record never offers the photo or video itself. Only a share link from the owner can.

Share links

  • Whoever has the link can see what the owner allowed.That may include a preview, the original file, and the service receipt. The original is the exact file that was uploaded, with any details stored in it. Previews carry none. Treat a share link like the photo itself.
  • An included evidence file names the account.If the owner includes the evidence file, it contains a Certifyr account number and this app installation’s device ID, so anyone can tell those evidence files come from the same account.
  • Owners can turn a link off at any time.After that, the page shows that the link was turned off. Download links on the page are short-lived and stop working soon after.
  • Hosting is time-limited on the free plan.Uploaded originals are hosted for 7 days, then deleted. The evidence record remains.

Photos taken with the app

  • Photos aren’t sent unless you choose to upload.With evidence on, the app sends a small record — file fingerprint, time, device model — not your photo. Here is everything it contains.
  • The small record holds only capture facts.The file’s fingerprint (SHA-256), size, format and dimensions — and for videos, their length and whether they have sound; which camera and lens took it; the capture time as reported by the iPhone, and how long the record took to send; whether a video recording was interrupted; the iPhone model (such as “iPhone17,1”), iOS version and app version; and the IDs of your Certifyr account, the app’s App Attest key and the one-time capture session.
  • It never holds pixels or location.Nor thumbnails, EXIF data, file names, contacts or advertising identifiers.
  • Copies Certifyr makes carry an invisible watermark.Share copies, and the copy of a photo the app saves for sharing, carry an invisible watermark holding only a random code, so a posted copy can be recognized. The original file is never changed.
  • Recognize Posted Copies is off unless you turn it on.If you turn it on in the app, it uploads a small private preview of each new photo (at most 1024 pixels, with no location or other details) so copies of it can be recognized. The preview isn’t shown on any public page. Turning the setting off deletes all of these previews.
  • Setting up evidence sends a key record once.When evidence is first set up, the app also sends a random ID for this installation of the app and Apple’s App Attest attestation for its key.
  • No training on your media.Certifyr doesn’t use your photos or videos to train anything.

Your account

  • Sign in with Apple, without your name or email.The app asks Apple for neither. Apple gives Certifyr an account ID, which keeps your records, uploads and settings together.
  • A Sign in with Apple token, stored encrypted.When you sign in, Certifyr keeps a token from Apple, encrypted, only so it can ask Apple to end Certifyr’s access to your Apple Account when you delete your account. It’s erased after that.
  • Your settings.Whether your records can be looked up by default, and whether Recognize Posted Copies is on, are stored with your account.
  • A public profile, only if you add one.A name and links you choose, shown as “Shared by” on your public photos only when you turn on “Show on my photos”. You can change or remove it at any time, and deleting your account deletes it.

Who else handles your data

Certifyr uses a few providers to run the service. They handle data only on Certifyr’s behalf and must protect it at least as well as this policy describes. Certifyr doesn’t sell your data or share it for advertising. Certifyr is run from the United States, and your data is stored and processed there.

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS)Runs Certifyr’s servers and database and stores the originals and previews you upload while they’re hosted, so it handles everything the app and the website send, including IP addresses.
  • GitHubHosts this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use and the Support page (GitHub Pages), and receives the IP address of anyone who opens them.
  • GoogleHandles the email you send to our support address (Gmail).
  • AppleHandles Sign in with Apple, and App Attest, which lets Certifyr check that evidence comes from the Certifyr app. Apple’s privacy policy covers what Apple receives.
  • OpenTimestamps calendarsReceive only a 32-byte fingerprint of each checkpoint of Certifyr’s public log, to timestamp it in Bitcoin. No photo, record, account or personal data is sent. The log itself publishes only fingerprints and counts; the entry for a deleted record keeps its fingerprint, and its details are erased with the record.

Deleting your data

  • One photo or video.Deleting it in the app removes it from your iPhone and from Certifyr: its hosted original and preview are deleted, its share links stop working, and it can’t be looked up anymore.
  • Your whole account.In the app, tap the thumbnail next to the shutter, then All at the top right (if a photo opens), then Settings (the gear), then Signed in with Apple at the top, then Delete Account. This signs you out everywhere, stops evidence recording on your devices, deletes your hosted originals and previews, turns off your share links, stops all your records from being looked up, and asks Apple to end Certifyr’s Sign in with Apple access. Photos and videos on your iPhone stay there until you delete them.
  • What Certifyr keeps.Deleted records, and a closed account’s records, device keys and Apple account ID, are erased 30 days after deletion; until then no one can look them up. A record under review after a report, or restricted after one, is kept until the review ends or while the restriction stands. For a suspended account, or one whose content was restricted, only a one-way code of its Apple account ID is kept, so the block can’t be undone by signing up again. Check results are deleted within about an hour.

Your choices and rights

  • In the app.You choose whether evidence is recorded, whether each record can be looked up, who gets a share link, whether Recognize Posted Copies is on, and whether a public profile is shown. You can delete any photo or video, or your whole account, at any time.
  • By email.You can ask for a copy of the data Certifyr holds about your account, or ask us to correct or delete it. Certifyr doesn’t know your name or email, so include a Record ID from your account (in the app: Info, then Technical Details). We answer within 30 days.
  • No selling, no ads.We don’t sell your personal information or share it for advertising, and Certifyr shows no ads and does no tracking.

Keeping your data safe

Connections to Certifyr are encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in sessions and share links are stored only as one-way codes, and the Sign in with Apple token is stored encrypted. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we’ll tell you as the law requires.

Children

Certifyr isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child under 13 has used Certifyr, email us and we’ll delete the data.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top. For important changes, we’ll also tell you in the app.
