The service

  • An iPhone camera and a checker.The Certifyr app saves the photos and videos you take on your iPhone and, if you choose, sends a small evidence record for each one. The app and the certifyr.app website let anyone check whether a file exactly matches a registered record.
  • Free, and in beta.Certifyr costs nothing today. While it’s in beta, features, limits and availability can change, and parts of it may be paused or discontinued. When a change affects you, we’ll say so in the app or on the website.
  • Daily uploads.Taking photos and videos is unlimited. You can upload 2 a day, reset at midnight UTC; a photo counts as 1 and a video up to 15 seconds counts as 2. Failed uploads and retries don’t count again, and checking a file never does.
  • Payment never changes evidence.If paid options are ever offered, they’ll be explained before you pay, and they’ll never change what any evidence or result says.

What a result means — and doesn’t

  • Results describe evidence, not the truth of a scene.“App-attested capture” means a file was recorded by the Certifyr camera app on an Apple device that passed App Attest. It doesn’t certify what the scene shows, who took it, or whether the scene was staged. Capture times come from the device’s own clock.
  • No match isn’t a verdict.Most genuine photos and videos are never registered. A file without a matching record isn’t shown to be fake, generated or edited.
  • Use results as one piece of information.Don’t rely on Certifyr alone for important decisions. Weigh a result together with your own judgment and other sources.

How Certifyr works

Your account

  • You must be at least 13.And old enough where you live to agree to these terms yourself.
  • Sign in with Apple.You need an account to record evidence and upload originals; you don’t need one to take photos or to check files. Keep your iPhone and Apple Account secure: what happens on your signed-in devices counts as yours.
  • You can delete your account at any time.In the app, tap the thumbnail next to the shutter, then All at the top right (if a photo opens), then Settings (the gear), then Signed in with Apple at the top, then Delete Account. What happens when you do

Your photos and videos

  • They stay yours.You keep every right you have in what you capture. Certifyr doesn’t claim ownership and doesn’t use your photos or videos to train anything.
  • What uploading allows us to do.When you upload an original, you let Certifyr store it, fingerprint it, make a preview of it, and show them to the people you share a link with — only to run the service, and only while it’s hosted.
  • Exact-file lookup is on by default.Anyone who has the exact same file can look up its record — never the photo or video itself. You can turn this off for each photo or video, or for all of them, in the app.
  • You decide who gets a share link.Anyone with a share link sees what you allowed: evidence, and if you chose, a preview or the original. You can turn a link off at any time.
  • You’re responsible for what you share.Only upload and share what you have the right to, with the consent of the people in it where the law requires.

Hosting and deletion
